Etubu Premium — App Review Notes

Product ID: com.etubu.premium
Type: Non-Consumable (one-time unlock)
Price: 249 TRY (Turkey) / StoreKit shows localized App Store price

What Premium unlocks
- All cluster themes and wallpapers (free tier: Tesla theme only)
- Live MapKit background
- Route planning (radar / corridor / chargers / weather along route)
- Live radar & average-speed corridor alerts
Free tier still shows the OSM road speed-limit sign while driving.

How to test
1. Open the app and accept the legal screen if shown.
2. Tap Settings (gear).
3. Open the Premium section at the top.
4. Tap “Sign in with Apple” (or purchase will prompt Sign in with Apple first).
5. Tap Unlock / Buy to purchase com.etubu.premium with a Sandbox Apple ID.
6. After purchase, Premium should show as active; themes, map toggle, and route planning unlock.
7. Optional restore test: delete and reinstall the app → Settings → Premium → Restore purchase (same Sandbox Apple ID). Entitlement returns via StoreKit.

Notes for reviewers
- Sign in with Apple is required before purchase so the account is linked in-app; the StoreKit entitlement is tied to the Apple ID and restores after reinstall.
- No account/password is required beyond Sign in with Apple and a Sandbox App Store account.
- IAP review screenshot shows the in-app Premium settings / unlock UI.
